## Things I've built

**[SonicTouch](https://github.com/SusmitaSahu365/sonictouch-flutter)** · Assistive tech · Flutter + Flask + CNN &nbsp;·&nbsp; [GitHub](https://github.com/SusmitaSahu365/sonictouch-flutter) · [Backend](https://github.com/SusmitaSahu365/sonictouch-backend) · [APK](https://github.com/SusmitaSahu365/sonictouch/releases/latest)

A sound-alert app for deaf and hard-of-hearing users. It listens in the background every 4 seconds, runs audio through a CNN I trained on UrbanSound8K, and fires a full-screen visual alert + vibration pattern when it detects something like a dog bark, car horn, or siren. Users can also register their own sounds — a doorbell, a specific alarm — and the app matches those using MFCC cosine similarity. The model hits 88–90% validation accuracy on 10 classes.

`Flutter` `TensorFlow` `Flask` `Firebase` `Librosa` `UrbanSound8K`

**[MediLingua](https://github.com/SusmitaSahu365/MediLingua)** · Healthcare AI · Flutter + FastAPI + AssemblyAI + Llama 3 &nbsp;·&nbsp; [GitHub](https://github.com/SusmitaSahu365/MediLingua) · [APK](https://github.com/SusmitaSahu365/MediLingua/releases/latest)

Doctors in multilingual environments have a real problem: consultations happen in regional languages, but records must be in English. MediLingua records the consultation, diarizes speakers via AssemblyAI, translates the transcript with Llama 3 on Groq, and generates a structured clinical summary — symptoms, diagnosis, recommendations — ready to store. The whole pipeline runs from a Flutter app backed by FastAPI, MySQL, and Firebase.

`Flutter` `FastAPI` `AssemblyAI` `Llama 3 / Groq` `MySQL` `Firebase`
